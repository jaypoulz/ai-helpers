# Red Hat Engineering Competencies - Quick Reference
**Source:** Engineering Competencies v9.0 (Mar 23, 2022)

## Four Main Categories

### 1. Technical Contribution
Competency subcategories for technical work and innovation:

- **Business Impact** - Direct/visible impact on business, cost reduction, customer needs
- **Scope** - Range from task → component → technical area → multiple areas
- **Evidence/Record** - Timely delivery, consistent contributions, high value output
- **Planning & Execution** - Self-sufficient execution → feature planning → forward planning
- **Opportunity Recognition** - Identifying emerging tech, new capabilities, patents
- **Creativity & Innovation** - Component improvements → impactful → recognized innovations
- **Technical Knowledge** - Entry → developing → practitioner → authority
- **Speaking/Publicity** - Scope and audience of external engagement

### 2. Leadership
Competency subcategories for influence and impact:

- **Work Impact** - Task → workload → features → functional area → product
- **Continuous Improvement** - Following → sharing → shaping → steering processes
- **Portfolio Impact** - Aware → applies → integrates → influences → impacts
- **Collaboration** - Developing → proficient → advancing → expert collaboration

### 3. Mentorship
Competency subcategories for developing others and self:

- **Growth Impact** - Who you mentor: mentee → team → groups → organization
- **Execution as a Mentee** - How you develop: guided → self-sufficient → planning → connecting to business

### 4. End-to-End Delivery
Competency subcategories for product delivery and customer focus:

- **Product Delivery Life Cycle** - Following → sharing → shaping → guiding delivery practices
- **Customer Involvement & Focus** - Awareness → attention → engagement → advocacy

## Mapping Accomplishments to Competencies

### Technical Work
**Bug fixes:**
- Technical Contribution: Business Impact, Scope
- End-to-End Delivery: Product Delivery Life Cycle

**New features/capabilities:**
- Technical Contribution: Creativity & Innovation, Scope
- Technical Contribution: Opportunity Recognition (if emerging tech)

**Refactoring/improvements:**
- Technical Contribution: Creativity & Innovation, Technical Knowledge
- Leadership: Continuous Improvement

**Technical design/architecture:**
- Technical Contribution: Scope, Technical Knowledge
- Leadership: Work Impact

### Collaboration & Cross-team Work
**Cross-team projects:**
- Leadership: Collaboration, Portfolio Impact

**Upstream contributions:**
- Leadership: Collaboration
- Technical Contribution: Scope, Evidence/Record

**API design with other teams:**
- Leadership: Collaboration
- Technical Contribution: Planning & Execution

### Process & Quality Improvements
**CI/CD improvements:**
- End-to-End Delivery: Product Delivery Life Cycle
- Leadership: Continuous Improvement

**Test infrastructure:**
- End-to-End Delivery: Product Delivery Life Cycle
- Technical Contribution: Creativity & Innovation

**Process improvements:**
- Leadership: Continuous Improvement
- Leadership: Work Impact

### Customer & Business Impact
**Customer issue resolution:**
- End-to-End Delivery: Customer Involvement & Focus
- Technical Contribution: Business Impact

**Customer engagement:**
- End-to-End Delivery: Customer Involvement & Focus
- Leadership: Collaboration

**Documentation/enablement:**
- End-to-End Delivery: Product Delivery Life Cycle
- Leadership: Work Impact

### Innovation & Strategy
**Patents/research:**
- Technical Contribution: Opportunity Recognition, Creativity & Innovation

**New technology adoption:**
- Technical Contribution: Opportunity Recognition
- Leadership: Continuous Improvement

**Strategic planning:**
- Leadership: Work Impact, Portfolio Impact
- Technical Contribution: Planning & Execution

## Level Indicators (for context)

### Senior Software Engineer (SSE)
- Scope: Technical Area
- Impact: Direct Impact
- Work: Major Features, Subject Matter Expert
- Collaboration: Advancing a product

### Principal Software Engineer (PSE)
- Scope: Multiple Technical Areas
- Impact: Visible Impact (recognized outside immediate team)
- Work: Functional Area leadership
- Collaboration: Advanced Cross-Functionally

### Senior Principal Software Engineer (SPSE)
- Scope: Architectural Coordination
- Impact: Revenue Impact
- Work: Major Segment of Product/Platform
- Collaboration: Expert at Product/Platform Segment

### Distinguished Engineer (DE)
- Scope: Department level influence
- Impact: Lasting Impact Across Products
- Work: Product/Platform leadership
- Collaboration: Expert across Products/Platforms

## Key Terms (from Glossary)

**Component** - Isolated, encapsulated unit of software (e.g., adapter, plugin, rpm package)

**Subsystem** - Major technical area involving multiple components (e.g., storage subsystem, network subsystem)

**Technical Area** - Major project or collection of related projects (e.g., Kernel, IdM, Ansible)

**Product** - Collection of supported software delivered to customers (e.g., RHEL, OpenShift, Satellite)

**Portfolio** - Set of related products spanning BUs (e.g., RHEL + OCP + Ansible)

**Cross-functional** - Collaborating across engineering and non-engineering functions (QE, Docs, PM, CEE, etc.)

**Initiative** - Strategic priority involving improvement across multiple projects/products

## Usage Notes

1. **Multiple competencies per accomplishment** - Most accomplishments map to 2-3 competency subcategories
2. **Primary vs secondary** - Identify the primary competency demonstrated, secondary competencies support it
3. **Level-appropriate** - Consider what level the work demonstrates (component vs subsystem vs product)
4. **Quantify impact** - Use the Business Impact and Evidence/Record categories to show measurable results
5. **Collaboration emphasis** - Red Hat values open collaboration - highlight cross-functional work
